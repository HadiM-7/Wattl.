# How to Use Your Animated Wattl Logo

## 1. Gmail Profile Picture

Use `profile-picture.gif` or `wave-profile-picture.gif` (250x250).

### Steps:
1. Open **Gmail** in your browser
2. Click your **profile icon** (top-right corner)
3. Click the **camera icon** on your current photo
4. Click **"Change"**
5. Select the **"From Computer"** tab
6. Click **"Upload from computer"** and select your chosen GIF
7. Adjust the crop if needed, then save
8. Wait **a few minutes** for it to propagate across Google services

> **Note:** The animation will show in email threads and anywhere your Google profile photo appears (Google Meet, Google Chat, etc.).

---

## 2. Gmail Email Signature

Use `email-signature.gif` or `wave-email-signature.gif` (500x500).

### Steps:
1. Open **Gmail** in your browser
2. Click the **gear icon** (top-right) → **"See all settings"**
3. Scroll down to the **"Signature"** section
4. Click **"+ Create new"** (or edit an existing signature)
5. In the signature editor, click the **image icon** (mountain/photo icon in the toolbar)
6. Select the **"Upload"** tab
7. Click **"Select a file from your device"** and choose your GIF
8. Once inserted, click the image in the editor to resize it:
   - **Small** (~100px) — subtle, professional
   - **Medium** (~200px) — good balance of visibility
   - Pick whichever feels right for your brand
9. Add any text you want alongside it (name, title, website, etc.)
10. Scroll to the bottom and click **"Save Changes"**

### Recommended signature layout:
```
[Animated Logo]
Hadi Mahmood
Wattl
yourwebsite.com
```

### Tips:
- Gmail will auto-host the image — no need for external hosting
- The GIF will animate in most email clients (Outlook desktop is a notable exception — it shows only the first frame)
- Keep the display size reasonable (100-200px wide) so it doesn't overwhelm your signature
- Test by sending yourself an email to preview how it looks
